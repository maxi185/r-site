A Letter for You

✦

Always & Forever

My dearest Amina,

There are moments — quiet ones, the kind the world never notices — when I look at you and feel something so overwhelming that language seems inadequate. No sentence could hold the entirety of what you mean to me. And yet, I keep trying, because you deserve to hear it over and over again.

You came into my life softly, the way dawn arrives — unhurried, inevitable, and so brilliantly warm that I could not imagine the world without your light ever again. Before you, I had grown accustomed to a certain ordinary quiet. You turned that quiet into music.

I love the way you laugh when something catches you off guard. I love the furrow of your brow when you're thinking deeply. I love your courage — the kind you don't even notice you carry, the kind that makes everyone around you feel braver just by being near you.

I want you to know that you are seen. Every version of you — the radiant and the weary, the certain and the doubting. I see them all and I choose them all, entirely and without reservation.

This website is a small and imperfect attempt at saying what my heart already knows in full: that loving you is the most extraordinary thing I have ever had the privilege of doing, and I would choose you in every life, in every timeline, without a single hesitation.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being mine. Thank you for making this life the most beautiful story I could have ever dreamed of living.

Yours, completely — always.
